# bostris

bostris is a falling-block puzzle game that runs entirely inside the terminal. It is made for players who want a quick, responsive game without leaving the command line, for those who want to sharpen their technique, and for anyone looking for a challenge against a computer opponent.

---

## How it plays

- Clear lines on a standard 10×20 field with all seven classic pieces
- Rotate with the modern rotation system, including wall kicks and 180° spins
- Plan ahead with a queue of up to six upcoming pieces and a hold slot
- See exactly where a piece will land with the ghost piece
- Soft drop or hard drop pieces into place
- Earn more for multi-line clears, T-spins, back-to-back clears, combos and perfect clears
- Face faster gravity as the level rises every ten lines

## Versus AI

- Battle a computer opponent that plays by the same rules and the same pieces as you
- Choose from seven difficulty levels, from a relaxed D up to a blistering X
- Send garbage lines to your opponent with strong clears, and cancel incoming garbage by clearing first
- Watch incoming attacks build up in a meter beside your board
- Be the last one standing to win the match

## Practise and improve

- Finesse tracking shows whether each piece was placed with the fewest possible inputs
- Accuracy and fault counts appear live in the stats panel
- Retry mode sends a badly placed piece back to the top to try again
- Your top ten scores are saved between sessions

## Make it yours

- Rebind every control from an in-game settings screen
- Tune DAS, ARR, soft drop speed and lock delay to match your handling
- Choose the starting level and how many upcoming pieces to show
- Turn the ghost piece on or off and pick a color palette

## Feels like a finished game

- Colorful pieces with true color support and fallbacks for simpler terminals
- A layout that adapts to the terminal size and scales up on large screens
- Pause, restart and game-over screens, with automatic pausing when the window loses focus
- Uses almost no CPU while idle and only a few megabytes of memory
